**Deal / players.** India's National Investment and Infrastructure Fund announced the first close of NIIF Infrastructure Fund II at INR 19,000 crore, about $2bn, more than 60% of its $3.2bn target size. NIIF also expects to mobilize roughly $950m of co-investment capital alongside the fund.

**Why it matters.** A sovereign-anchored close of this size, even mid-target, signals institutional capital is still willing to commit fresh money to infrastructure despite a choppier Western fundraising market. It is a data point on how much dry powder is stacking up for the same power, transport and digital assets global sponsors are chasing in Asia.

**Why you care.** A useful fundraising comp for how much capital is chasing similar assets outside MSIP's core OECD footprint.
