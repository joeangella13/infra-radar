### IFM's North America infrastructure head to retire after 18 years; firm announces ABP majority stake from CPP and OMERS

`2026-10-22` · Sponsor moves · [Financial Standard](https://www.financialstandard.com.au/news/ifm-continues-global-infra-drive-with-appointment-acquisition-179814353)

**Deal / players.** Financial Standard (Oct 22) reports IFM Investors' head of infrastructure for North America will retire after an 18-year tenure. In the same piece, IFM, via a managed fund, is described as acquiring a majority stake in Associated British Ports from CPP Investments (33.9%) and OMERS (30%), who have agreed to sell. Price is not given in this source.

**Why it matters.** A senior exit at a large core investor can change deal appetite and relationships in North America; the successor was not named in the piece I read. On ABP, two Canadian pensions selling together gives a fresh control-stake comp for a UK port, but this source gives no price. Watch for the successor and how IFM funds the deal.

**Why you care.** IFM is a core-infrastructure rival, so a change at the top of its North America team and a UK port control deal are both worth tracking for competing bids and relationships.
