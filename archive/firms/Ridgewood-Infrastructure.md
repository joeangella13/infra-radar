### Ridgewood Infrastructure acquires Dauntless Air, operator of North America's largest Fire Boss scooper fleet

`2026-06-10` · Sponsor moves · [Ridgewood Infrastructure](https://ridgewoodinfrastructure.com/ridgewood-infrastructure-acquires-dauntless-air-premier-provider-of-emergency-management-infrastructure/)

**Deal / players.** Ridgewood Infrastructure LLC acquired Dauntless Air, an aerial firefighting company providing wildfire suppression under long-term government contracts. Terms undisclosed and the seller was not disclosed. Dauntless operates North America's largest Fire Boss fleet of Single Engine Scoopers, working with federal and state agencies on availability-based contracts that include pre-positioning and re-positioning during fire seasons. Ridgewood cited essential services, mission-critical operations, strong barriers to entry and operational growth potential, with plans to expand fleet and adjacent services.

**Why it matters.** The economics turn on availability-based contracting: Dauntless is paid standby rates whether or not the aircraft fly, with flight-hour revenue on top, so the cash flow is contracted rather than the volatile fee-for-service the sector looks like. Barriers are real, since amphibious aircraft, agency certifications, trained crews and narrow approved-vendor lists all take time. Terms and seller undisclosed.

**Why you care.** Availability-based contracting turning a services business into contracted cash flow is a boundary test for what counts as infrastructure, but terms and seller are undisclosed and it sits outside your coverage.
