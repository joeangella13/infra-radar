### Antin sells 30% of Norwegian wellboat operator Sølvtrans to GLIL Infrastructure

`2026-07-29` · Sponsor moves · [BusinessWire via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/small-business/articles/antin-sell-minority-stake-lvtrans-050000795.html)

**Deal / players.** Antin Infrastructure Partners' Flagship Fund III agreed to sell a 30% stake in Sølvtrans to UK investment fund GLIL Infrastructure, with Antin retaining majority ownership alongside founder Roger Halsebakk and his family; terms undisclosed. Ålesund-headquartered Sølvtrans is the largest provider of wellboats to the aquaculture industry, operating 48 vessels with eight more under construction, up from 21 vessels when Antin invested in 2018.
