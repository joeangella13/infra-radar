### IFM completes Atlas Arteria takeover, reaching 67.43% voting power in the toll road group

`2026-07-07` · Sponsor moves · [Kalkine](https://kalkine.com.au/news/announcements/ifm-global-infrastructure-fund-completes-takeover-of-atlas-arteria-with-6743-voting-control), [Atlas Arteria](https://www.atlasarteria.com/investor-centre/ifm-takeover-offer)

**Deal / players.** IFM Global Infrastructure Fund's bid vehicle Diamond Infraco 1 closed its off-market offer for Atlas Arteria on 7 July 2026 with 67.43% voting power, including acceptances settling up to 21 days later. The offer began with a bidder's statement on 27 April 2026, was initially rejected by the Atlas Arteria board on 6 May, and was raised in June before the board recommended it. Atlas Arteria owns 30.8% of the 2,424km APRR/AREA/A79/ADELAC French motorway network, 66.67% of the Chicago Skyway, a 100% economic interest in the Dulles Greenway and 100% of Germany's Warnow Tunnel.

**Why it matters.** IFM's bid vehicle closed its off-market offer for Atlas Arteria at 67.43% voting power, enough to drive distribution policy and refinancing without compulsory acquisition. The board rejected the offer on 6 May before IFM raised it in June and recommended it. The prize is the 30.8% APRR stake, inflation-linked French motorway tolls. Dulles Greenway, with capped tolls and traffic below underwriting, is the problem asset.

**Why you care.** A rejected-then-recommended take-private of a listed toll road vehicle is strong why-this-fund material on how core infrastructure buyers price concession cash flows.
