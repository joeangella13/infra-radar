### Hydrostor raises $230m equity round to push Willow Rock CAES project toward financial close

`2026-08-14` · Tracked situations · [Hydrostor](https://hydrostor.ca/hydrostor-secures-230-million-to-progress-advanced-compressed-air-energy-storage-projects/), [No2NuclearPower](https://www.no2nuclearpower.org.uk/news/us-energy-storage-14-8-26/)

**Deal / players.** Hydrostor secured a $230m equity investment combining a previously committed convertible note from Canada Growth Fund, Goldman Sachs Alternatives and CPP Investments with new strategic equity from Baker Hughes, Hatch and Realize Capital Partners. The funding backs Hydrostor's 7GW global compressed-air storage pipeline and is meant to carry the flagship 500MW Willow Rock project in California to financial close later this year.

**Why it matters.** Willow Rock financial close is the milestone that turns Hydrostor's compressed-air technology from permitted project into a bankable asset class other long-duration storage developers will be marked against. Strategic money from an oilfield services player like Baker Hughes alongside pension and bank capital signals the round was oversubscribed on credibility, not just balance-sheet need.

**Why you care.** Your Rothschild-era Hydrostor thesis gets a fresh proof point — watch whether Willow Rock's financial close terms become the long-duration storage comp MSIP marks against.
