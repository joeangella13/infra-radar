### Brightline Florida wins fifth AAFOH and 14th commuter indenture extension, pushing payments to July 30-31

`2026-07-27` · Tracked situations · [Bond Buyer](https://www.bondbuyer.com/news/another-debt-extension-for-brightline-florida)

**Deal / players.** Holders of Brightline Florida's $1.2bn unrated tax-exempt AAFOH bonds and $985m commuter bonds agreed to move the AAFOH debt payment to July 31 and the commuter mandatory redemption to July 30, with commuter interest also due July 31. Bond Buyer reports this is the fifth AAFOH supplement and the 14th commuter indenture supplement. Reported holders include First Eagle, Nuveen, Invesco, BlackRock and Macquarie; Assured Guaranty insures roughly 51% of the $2.2bn senior bonds, and hedge funds hold $1.1bn of subordinate taxable notes against total debt of about $5.5bn.

**Why it matters.** Extensions have compressed from months to days, a fifth AAFOH supplement and a fourteenth commuter one, which says no consensual deal is documented and creditors are pricing optionality week by week. Assured Guaranty, insuring roughly 51% of the $2.2bn senior bonds, effectively controls whether this stays out of court. Commuter holders are the most exposed, since their security rests on county payments that are not yet contractually committed.

**Why you care.** The creditor map on your own deal: who holds what and who has control. Exactly the detail that makes a restructuring walkthrough credible.

### Macquarie completes $40bn exit of Aligned Data Centers to AIP, MGX and GIP consortium after eight-year hold

**Deal / players.** Macquarie Asset Management completed the sale of Aligned Data Centers to a consortium comprising the AI Infrastructure Partnership, MGX and BlackRock's Global Infrastructure Partners. The transaction was announced in October 2025 at a US$40bn enterprise value. Under Macquarie's eight-year ownership from 2018, Aligned grew from two facilities with 85MW to 51 campuses and more than 6.4GW of operational and planned capacity across the Americas; returns to MAM were not disclosed.

Over eight years Macquarie took Aligned from two facilities and 85MW to 51 campuses and more than 6.4GW. Returns to MAM undisclosed.

### AD Ports buys Brazilian agri-bulk terminal operator CLI for $835m enterprise value from Macquarie and IG4

`2026-06-02` · Sector themes · [AD Ports Group](https://www.adportsgroup.com/en/news-and-media/2026/06/02/ad-ports-group-acquires-cli), [Port Technology International](https://www.porttechnology.org/ad-ports-acquires-brazilian-agri-bulk-terminal-operator/)

**Deal / players.** AD Ports Group agreed to acquire CLI for AED 3.1 billion (USD 835 million) enterprise value from Macquarie Asset Management and IG4 Capital, taking 80% of CLI Sul — Brazil's leading sugar export terminal at the Port of Santos — and 100% of CLI Norte, a grains gateway at the Port of Itaqui. The assets handled 17 million tonnes of agri-bulk in 2025 on revenue of USD 178 million and EBITDA of USD 98 million. It is AD Ports' largest acquisition to date, surpassing the 2023 Noatum purchase.

**Why it matters.** An $835m enterprise value on $98m of EBITDA is roughly 8.5x trailing, a rare disclosed mark for Latin American agri-bulk terminals where comparables are scarce. It is also a completed sponsor exit: Macquarie and IG4 sold a Brazilian port platform to a sovereign-backed strategic at institutional pricing. Read the multiple as bulk-appropriate, since sugar and grain export volumes carry commodity-cycle throughput risk that container gateways do not.

**Why you care.** A live ports comp for terminal pitches, and evidence of what a sponsor exit to a Gulf strategic clears at, which is relevant to your Odfjell and LOGISTEC work.

### Temasek, GIC and CalPERS revealed as co-investors in Macquarie-led A$11.7bn Qube take-private

`2026-05-14` · Sponsor moves · [Infrastructure Investor](https://www.infrastructureinvestor.com/temasek-gic-calpers-revealed-as-investors-in-macquarie-led-bid-to-acquire-qube/), [MSN / AFR](https://www.msn.com/en-au/money/economy/supreme-court-approves-11-7b-macquarie-asset-management-led-takeover-of-qube/ar-AA27qPOG)

**Deal / players.** Infrastructure Investor identified Temasek, GIC and CalPERS as co-investors in the Macquarie Asset Management-led consortium bidding for Australian ports and logistics operator Qube Holdings. The scheme was agreed in February 2026 at about A$11.7bn (roughly US$8.3bn) enterprise value, A$5.20 per share in cash, via MAM's MAIF 4 vehicle. The New South Wales Supreme Court subsequently approved the scheme in July 2026.

**Why it matters.** The largest Australian infrastructure take-private of the cycle, at roughly A$11.7bn enterprise value and A$5.20 a share in cash, shows listed logistics platforms with landside port concessions still clear at a premium when sovereign and pension money syndicates behind a manager. That co-investment is what allows a cheque this size without a GP club. The NSW court approved the scheme in July.

**Why you care.** Benchmark for how a large ports-and-logistics platform gets funded, and a concrete example of co-invest structure if fund strategy comes up.
