### Meridiam refinances Netcity Telecom with EUR80m, Romania's first project finance bond at 26.5-year tenor

`2026-07-23` · Sponsor moves · [Meridiam](https://www.meridiam.com/news/successful-refinancing-of-netcity-telecom-bucharests-digital-infrastructure-backbone/)

**Deal / players.** Meridiam completed an EUR 80 million refinancing of Netcity Telecom, Bucharest's underground telecommunications network, with tenor of up to 26.5 years described as a new market record and the first project finance bond issuance in Romania. Lenders comprised an international institutional investor, an international bank and a local Romanian bank. Netcity operates over 3,000km of underground telecoms infrastructure serving approximately 70,000 buildings and nearly 600,000 homes and offices under a 49-year municipal concession running since 2008. Meridiam holds 80% and E-INFRA Group 20%.

**Why it matters.** Meridiam refinanced Netcity Telecom, Bucharest's underground telecoms network, with EUR80m at up to 26.5 years, described as Romania's first project finance bond. Netcity runs over 3,000km of duct serving nearly 600,000 homes and offices under a 49-year municipal concession dating to 2008, exactly the monopoly profile that supports very long amortising debt. The bigger win is a Romanian project bond benchmark others can reuse across CEE.

**Why you care.** Handy long-tenor financing precedent for concession assets. Otherwise general market colour on CEE digital infrastructure, not something you can act on.

### Meridiam leads EUR23m investment in Chargepoly for heavy-duty EV fleet charging infrastructure

`2026-07-22` · Sponsor moves · [Meridiam](https://www.meridiam.com/news/meridiam-leads-a-e23m-investment-in-chargepoly-to-accelerate-the-deployment-of-charging-solutions-for-heavy-duty-electric-vehicles/)

**Deal / players.** Meridiam, through its Green Impact Growth Fund, led a EUR 23 million investment in Chargepoly alongside Fideve Groupe. Founded in 2019 and headquartered in Aix-en-Provence, Chargepoly designs and operates DC charging infrastructure for heavy-duty vehicle fleets across France, the UK and Canada, combining modular charging hardware with power-allocation software called Lucie, tested across 50+ heavy-duty vehicle models. Proceeds fund international expansion and platform development. Customers include CMA-CGM, with OEM partnerships including Renault Trucks, Volvo and Daimler.

**Why it matters.** Meridiam led a EUR 23m investment in Chargepoly, which builds and operates DC charging for heavy-duty fleets across France, the UK and Canada. Depot charging beats public charging because utilisation is contracted against a known fleet, and Chargepoly's Lucie software shares one constrained grid connection across many charge points, avoiding the substation upgrades that kill most heavy-duty electrification cases. This is growth equity, not infrastructure yield.

**Why you care.** Small and early, but the grid-connection-as-scarce-asset framing travels well into any charging or fleet electrification pitch you build.

### Meridiam invests EUR5m in Blyyd to scale electric yard tractors for logistics operations

`2026-06-18` · Sponsor moves · [Meridiam](https://www.meridiam.com/news/meridiam-invests-e5m-in-blyyd-to-support-the-decarbonization-of-logistics-yard-operations/)

**Deal / players.** Meridiam invested EUR 5 million via its Green Impact Growth Fund in Blyyd, a Lyon-based company founded in 2015 that makes fully electric yard tractors for logistics and industrial operations. The technology uses swappable battery systems and smart monitoring designed for continuous high-intensity yard duty. Proceeds fund industrial ramp-up of new-generation tractors, commercial growth and European expansion. Blyyd has 160 tractors deployed across 100 sites serving major retailers and logistics providers.

**Why it matters.** Yard tractors electrify well because duty cycles are short-radius, high-hours and entirely on private property, so there is no public charging dependency and diesel displacement works without subsidy. Swappable batteries are the specific fix for three-shift yards with no charging window. At 160 tractors across 100 sites Blyyd has product validation but not manufacturing scale, so the EUR5m is early growth capital and the risk is industrial ramp.

**Why you care.** Small and early, but it is logistics-yard decarbonisation, adjacent to your LOGISTEC/Termont work and a marker of where venture-adjacent infra money is going.

### US public pension commitments to infrastructure funds fell to $17.4B across 165 commitments in 2025, down from $21.3B

`2026-06-01` · Market / regulatory · [Bond Buyer](https://www.bondbuyer.com/news/georgia-dominates-p3-space-last-year-while-pension-funds-continue-to-invest-in-infrastructure-funds)

**Deal / players.** The Reason Foundation's Annual Transportation Finance Report, covered by Bond Buyer on June 1, 2026, found US public pension commitments to infrastructure funds fell to 165 commitments totalling $17.4 billion in 2025, from 254 commitments totalling $21.3 billion in 2024 and 238 totalling $23.8 billion in 2023. Twenty-six states committed $13.3 billion, led by CalPERS at $1.8 billion, New Mexico at $1.4 billion, Washington at $1.3 billion and Connecticut at $1.2 billion. Report author Robert Poole said he was surprised by the decline and could not identify the driver.

**Why it matters.** US public pensions made 165 infrastructure fund commitments totalling $17.4bn in 2025, down from 254 worth $21.3bn in 2024 and 238 worth $23.8bn in 2023, per the Reason Foundation. Twenty-six states committed $13.3bn, led by CalPERS at $1.8bn. That is 35% fewer cheques and 18% less money, which points to capital concentrating in brand-name managers while mid-market and first-time funds face a tighter market.

**Why you care.** Fundraising context for a why-this-fund answer, where LP flows are narrowing toward the largest franchises. The report author could not explain the drop, so do not over-read it.

### Meridiam launches Siruai 100MW wind plus 50MWh storage project in Kenya, over EUR200m investment

`2026-05-11` · Sponsor moves · [Meridiam](https://www.meridiam.com/news/in-kenya-meridiam-launches-a-pioneering-wind-and-storage-project-a-first-in-east-africa/)

**Deal / players.** Meridiam launched the Siruai Wind and Storage project in Kajiado County, south of Nairobi, alongside Craftskills Energy and with Government of Kenya support. The project comprises 100MW of wind capacity plus 50MWh of battery storage, representing over EUR 200 million of investment, and sits adjacent to the existing Kipeto wind farm. It was launched at the Africa Forward summit on 11 May 2026 and is described as a first in East Africa for combining wind with battery storage. The project will double Meridiam's wind generation capacity in Kenya.

**Why it matters.** Meridiam launched Siruai, 100MW of wind plus 50MWh of storage in Kajiado County, over EUR200m of investment, with Craftskills Energy and Kenyan government support. Kenyan wind sells under long-term dollar PPAs with KPLC, so cash flow is contracted hard currency but carries offtaker credit risk. The storage firms up output and addresses curtailment, and siting next to Kipeto shares grid connection, land and O&M.

**Why you care.** General market colour rather than something actionable, though the wind-plus-storage firming argument is transferable if you need to defend storage value in a client pitch.
