### InfraVia to acquire D-Marin, EMEA premium marina platform, from CVC Capital Partners VII

`2026-07-06` · Sponsor moves · [CVC](https://www.cvc.com/media/news/2026/cvc-agrees-sale-of-d-marin-the-leading-premium-marina-operator-in-the-emea-region-to-infravia-capital-partners/), [InfraVia Capital Partners](https://infraviacapital.com/infravia-to-acquire-d-marin-a-leading-premium-marina-platform-in-the-mediterranean/)

**Deal / players.** InfraVia Capital Partners agreed to acquire D-Marin from CVC Capital Partners VII. Terms undisclosed. D-Marin operates 28 premium marinas across nine countries including Turkey, Croatia, Greece, UAE, Spain, Italy, France, Malta and Albania, with over 14,300 berths of which more than 1,000 are dedicated superyacht berths, plus 12 professional boatyards servicing over 2,500 yachts annually. CVC acquired the business in 2020 and built it into the regional market leader.

**Why it matters.** Marinas behave more like ports than leisure assets: concession or long-leasehold tenure, multi-year berth contracts, high renewal rates and effectively fixed supply because coastal permitting is near-impossible. InfraVia's levers are further consolidation of a fragmented market and yield management on the 1,000-plus superyacht berths where scarcity bites hardest. But CVC already ran the roll-up to 28 marinas in nine countries, so InfraVia is paying for a built platform. Terms undisclosed.

**Why you care.** Sponsor-to-sponsor exit in a niche ports-adjacent asset. Handy example if you are asked where infra funds find pricing power outside core sectors.

### InfraVia critical metals fund invests SEK420m for 6.6% of Viscaria copper project in northern Sweden

`2026-06-17` · Sponsor moves · [InfraVia Capital Partners](https://infraviacapital.com/infravia-announces-the-third-investment-of-its-critical-metals-fund-in-a-swedish-copper-project/)

**Deal / players.** InfraVia's Critical Metals Fund invested SEK 420 million (EUR 39 million) for a 6.6% stake in the Viscaria copper project at Kiruna, northern Sweden, developed by Nasdaq Stockholm-listed Gruvaktiebolaget Viscaria. This is the fund's third investment. The project is expected to produce approximately 26,000 tonnes of contained copper annually by 2028, with hydroelectric power, rail access and low CO2 emissions cited as operational advantages.

**Why it matters.** InfraVia's Critical Metals Fund put SEK 420m (EUR 39m) into a 6.6% stake in the Viscaria copper project at Kiruna, developed by Nasdaq Stockholm-listed Gruvaktiebolaget Viscaria, its third investment in the strategy. Viscaria targets roughly 26,000 tonnes of contained copper a year by 2028, helped by hydro power and rail access. Minority and pre-production, so returns ride on construction and copper price, not contracted income.

**Why you care.** Sits in your mining services lane and is a good test case for how far an infrastructure mandate now stretches, which is a question you should have an answer to.
