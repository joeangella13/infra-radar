### Partners Group wraps debut infrastructure secondaries effort at $5.5bn, seven times its original target

`2026-07-23` · Sponsor moves · [Infrastructure Investor](https://www.infrastructureinvestor.com/partners-group-wraps-up-debut-infra-secondaries-fundraising-effort-with-5-5bn-raised/)

**Deal / players.** Partners Group closed its debut infrastructure secondaries effort with $5.5bn raised in total, comprising $1.7bn in the Partners Group Infrastructure Secondary fund vehicle and $3.8bn in bespoke separate mandates. The original target was $750m. LP composition was not disclosed.

**Why it matters.** The split is the story: $3.8bn of bespoke mandates against a $1.7bn commingled fund raised on a $750m target. Large LPs want infrastructure secondaries exposure on their own terms and fee schedules, not through a blind pool. Raising more than seven times target confirms demand far exceeds deal supply, a mismatch that pushes pricing toward par and erodes the discount the strategy depends on.

**Why you care.** Fundraising-market context for interviews. Shows you track where LP capital is going and gives you a real view on why secondaries returns may compress.

### Partners Group closes fourth direct infrastructure programme at more than $15bn

`2026-07-20` · Sponsor moves · [Institutional Real Estate](https://irei.com/news/partners-group-surpasses-15b-for-fourth-direct-infrastructure-programme-in-final-close/), [Infrastructure Investor](https://www.infrastructureinvestor.com/partners-group-raises-15bn-for-its-fourth-direct-infra-programme/)

**Deal / players.** Partners Group held a final close on more than $15bn for its fourth direct infrastructure programme, focused on building next-generation energy, utility and other infrastructure platforms with an emphasis on mid-market opportunities. Investors included public and corporate pension plans, sovereign wealth funds, insurers, endowments, foundations, GPs, consultants and banks across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia Pacific. Deployment to date was not disclosed.

**Why it matters.** Partners Group closed its fourth direct infrastructure programme above $15bn, targeting next-generation energy and utility platforms with a mid-market emphasis; deployment undisclosed. Close to half sits in separate accounts rather than the commingled fund, which is how headline programme sizes keep growing while pension commitment counts fall. That is $15bn of fresh bid pressure on already crowded digital and power auctions.

**Why you care.** Competitive intel for processes you run, and the dual-vehicle structure is a sharp observation to offer when a fund asks what you notice in today's fundraising market.
