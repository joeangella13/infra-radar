### Brightline Florida wins fifth AAFOH and 14th commuter indenture extension, pushing payments to July 30-31

`2026-07-27` · Tracked situations · [Bond Buyer](https://www.bondbuyer.com/news/another-debt-extension-for-brightline-florida)

**Deal / players.** Holders of Brightline Florida's $1.2bn unrated tax-exempt AAFOH bonds and $985m commuter bonds agreed to move the AAFOH debt payment to July 31 and the commuter mandatory redemption to July 30, with commuter interest also due July 31. Bond Buyer reports this is the fifth AAFOH supplement and the 14th commuter indenture supplement. Reported holders include First Eagle, Nuveen, Invesco, BlackRock and Macquarie; Assured Guaranty insures roughly 51% of the $2.2bn senior bonds, and hedge funds hold $1.1bn of subordinate taxable notes against total debt of about $5.5bn.

**Why it matters.** Extensions have compressed from months to days, a fifth AAFOH supplement and a fourteenth commuter one, which says no consensual deal is documented and creditors are pricing optionality week by week. Assured Guaranty, insuring roughly 51% of the $2.2bn senior bonds, effectively controls whether this stays out of court. Commuter holders are the most exposed, since their security rests on county payments that are not yet contractually committed.

**Why you care.** Extensions compressed from months to days on your Brightline deal, and Assured Guaranty, insuring roughly 51% of the $2.2bn senior bonds, effectively controls whether this stays out of court.

### Delfin Midstream takes $5bn FID on first US floating LNG vessel, 4.4 mtpa, with GIP leading equity

**Deal / players.** Delfin Midstream announced a $5 billion final investment decision for FLNG 1, a 4.4 mtpa floating liquefaction vessel offshore Louisiana, on June 3, 2026 — the first floating liquefaction facility in the United States and the largest FLNG project globally. Equity came from Global Infrastructure Partners as lead, alongside Mitsui O.S.K. Lines, Vitol and Diameter Capital Partners. Long-term offtake is contracted with Vitol, Expand Energy, Centrica and Gunvor, with first LNG targeted for 2030 and full project capacity of up to 13.2 mtpa.

**Why it matters.** A $5bn FID for the first US floating liquefaction vessel, 4.4 mtpa offshore Louisiana, first LNG targeted for 2030 and full capacity up to 13.2 mtpa. Offshore FLNG sidesteps the onshore siting, permitting and construction-labour bottlenecks inflating Gulf Coast capex. The capital structure is the tell: GIP leading alongside a shipowner, a trader and a credit fund, financed against long-term offtake with Vitol, Expand, Centrica and Gunvor.

**Why you care.** The capital structure is the lesson: an infra fund, a shipowner, a trader and a credit fund underwriting $5bn against long-term offtake. Also a live US midstream comp.
