### Brookfield and NextEra to build $100bn AI data centre campus at DOE's Paducah site with 4.6GW of dedicated generation

`2026-07-29` · Sponsor moves · [POWER Magazine](https://www.powermag.com/brookfield-nextera-to-develop-100b-data-center-campus-at-does-paducah-site-paired-with-4-6-gw-of-dedicated-generation/), [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/brookfield-to-develop-gigawatt-scale-data-center-campus-at-does-kentucky-nuclear-enrichment-plant/)

**Deal / players.** Brookfield and NextEra Energy will develop a privately funded $100bn AI data centre campus at the Department of Energy's Paducah site in western Kentucky, selected from a DOE Request for Offers issued in November 2025. Brookfield develops and operates the data centre on long-term DOE land leases; NextEra owns and operates up to 4.6GW of dedicated generation comprising up to 2GW of natural gas and up to 2.6GW of battery storage, added in stages. Big Rivers Electric provides wholesale service and Jackson Purchase Energy Cooperative retail delivery; full buildout targets 2032 supporting up to 1.8GW of utility capacity and over 1.2GW of compute load, subject to Kentucky Public Service Commission approval.

**Why it matters.** DOE awarded Brookfield a lease to develop a data centre campus at the former Paducah Gaseous Diffusion Plant in Kentucky and selected NextEra for power: up to 1.8 GW of utility capacity, over 1.2 GW of compute and 4.6 GW of dedicated generation, split 2 GW gas and 2.6 GW storage, fully built by 2032 at a reported $100bn. No offtaker disclosed, so it is a commitment to build, not a contract.

**Why you care.** The largest live behind-the-fence gas-plus-storage structure on federal land, 4.6GW dedicated to one campus. No offtaker disclosed, so it is a commitment to build, not a contract.

### Blackstone, Brookfield and KKR sign $16bn Kuwait crude pipeline partnership, the country's largest ever FDI

`2026-07-28` · Sponsor moves · [Blackstone](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/kuwait-oil-company-signs-us-16-0-billion-infrastructure-partnership-involving-its-crude-oil-pipeline-network-with-a-consortium-comprising-blackstone-brookfield-and-kkr/), [Rigzone](https://www.rigzone.com/news/kuwait_agrees_16b_pipeline_leaseback_with_blackstone_brookfield_kkr-27-jul-2026-184217-article/)

**Deal / players.** Kuwait Oil Company signed a $16bn infrastructure partnership, codenamed Project Peregrine, with a consortium of Blackstone, Brookfield and KKR covering 13 crude oil pipelines spanning roughly 320km of Kuwait's domestic and export network. The consortium takes 49% of the joint venture in equal roughly 16.33% stakes, with KOC retaining 51% and operational control; KOC receives $7.85bn of upfront proceeds at closing. The JV grants KOC exclusive use and operating rights for 20.5 years in exchange for a volume-based tariff; KPC called it the largest foreign direct investment in Kuwait's history.

**Why it matters.** In substance a sale-and-leaseback: the sponsors own pipelines they do not operate and collect a volume-based tariff from a state oil company for 20.5 years, so the risk is throughput and convertibility rather than operations. A volume-linked rather than fixed tariff gives growth participation but removes the availability-payment floor infrastructure buyers usually insist on. KOC keeps 51% and control, taking $7.85bn upfront.

**Why you care.** Three sponsors splitting 49% equally is how country risk gets spread, on a structure that is really a sale-and-leaseback with a volume tariff and no availability floor.

**Deal / players.** Brookfield Infrastructure announced plans to merge BIP, its limited partnership, and BIPC, its corporate vehicle, into a single listed corporation, BIP Inc, on a one-for-one exchange for both unitholders and exchangeable shareholders. Management cited broadening the investor base, supporting index demand and removing partnership tax reporting. Special meetings are set for 14 October 2026 with a 21 August 2026 record date and completion expected in Q4 2026; the transaction is expected to be tax-deferred for Canadian and US investors.

**Why it matters.** Brookfield plans to collapse BIP and BIPC into one corporation, BIP Inc, one-for-one. The dual structure traded at a discount because index funds and many US taxable investors avoid K-1 partnerships, so this is valuation arbitrage rather than anything operational: index inclusion mechanically adds passive demand for the same cash flows. Special meetings are set for 14 October with completion expected in Q4 2026.

**Why you care.** Listed-comp housekeeping: a one-for-one collapse chasing index demand, relevant only if BIP sits in your trading comps set or you are sizing Brookfield's acquisition currency.

Brookfield finances the power projects through its AI Infrastructure Fund, launched in November 2025 with a $100 billion deployment target, while Bloom supplies its fuel cell platform. For infra investors it establishes onsite generation-as-a-service as a fundable, scaled asset class with a named anchor sponsor, and it creates a comparable for pricing similar platforms. Watch installed MW and contracted offtake disclosure — the dollar headline is a deployment target, not committed capital.

**Why you care.** Brookfield putting $25bn behind onsite power makes generation-as-a-service a fundable asset class and sets the comparable; the headline is a deployment target, so watch installed MW and contracted offtake.
