### Ares takes significant minority stake in Statera's 1.9 GW UK storage and flexible power portfolio

`2026-10-19` · Sponsor moves · [Mercom Capital Group](https://mercomcapital.com/ares-acquires-stake-in-stateras-1-9-gw-uk-energy-portfolio/)

**Deal / players.** Ares Infrastructure Equity funds acquired a significant minority stake in Statera Energy's operational portfolio of about 1.9 GW, including the 680 MW Carrington Storage project in Greater Manchester and the 750 MW Thurrock Flexible Generation project in Essex. It is Ares Infrastructure Equity's first investment in Europe. Statera keeps owning and operating the assets and continues to develop a 16 GW pipeline. Terms undisclosed; Macquarie Capital advised Statera.

**Why it matters.** Buying into operating assets avoids construction risk, and Statera stays as owner-operator with Ares as partner, with the stake supplying capital for its growth plans. Price was not disclosed, so there is no multiple to mark yet. Watch whether other large managers follow Ares into GB grid-balancing assets as renewables take a bigger share of supply.

**Why you care.** Ares' first European infrastructure equity cheque went into GB storage, a read-across for storage underwriting and for Hydrostor's long-duration pitch, though no price is out.
