### KKR and ECP agree £5.75bn take-private of DCC Energy at 6,525p per share

`2026-07-27` · Sponsor moves · [RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/3670685/dcc-energy-agrees-to-5-75-billion-takeover-by-kkr-energy-capital-partners.aspx), [Energynews.pro](https://energynews.pro/en/kkr-et-energy-capital-partners-rachetent-dcc-energy-pour-575-milliards-de-livres)

**Deal / players.** KKR and Energy Capital Partners, through Dragon Bidco, agreed to acquire DCC Energy for £5.75bn via a court-sanctioned scheme of arrangement at 6,525p per share in cash plus a 147.22p final dividend, a 24% premium to the 5,380p undisturbed price. Shareholders may receive up to a further 125p per share if the Nexora technology business is sold by 31 July 2027. An initial April bid at 5,800p was rejected before the consortium raised to 6,525p in June; the board unanimously recommended the improved terms and completion is expected in Q1 2027.

**Why it matters.** KKR and ECP agreed to take DCC Energy private for GBP5.75bn at 6,525p a share plus a 147.22p final dividend, a 24% premium to the 5,380p undisturbed price, with up to 125p more if Nexora is sold by 31 July 2027; an April bid at 5,800p was rejected. DCC distributes LPG and liquid fuels on unit margins, so returns must come from bolt-ons and margin, not re-rating. Completion expected Q1 2027.

**Why you care.** Take-private mechanics worth having cold: two-stage bid, 24% premium, contingent consideration on a non-core asset. Standard interview territory, and ECP appears twice in this batch.
