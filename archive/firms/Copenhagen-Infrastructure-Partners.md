### Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners targets €16bn for sixth flagship renewables fund

`2026-06-19` · Sponsor moves · [Infrastructure Investor](https://www.infrastructureinvestor.com/cip-eyes-e16bn-for-latest-renewables-flagship-exclusive/)

**Deal / players.** Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners is targeting €16bn for CI VI, its sixth flagship renewables vehicle. The predecessor, CI V, closed above its €12bn target in March 2025. First close timing and terms were not disclosed.

**Why it matters.** A EUR 16bn target is a one-third step-up on a predecessor that closed above its EUR 12bn target, and it tests whether greenfield renewables can still absorb mega-fund capital as US tax credits sunset and European auction pricing resets. CIP creates value development-to-COD, so returns hinge on locking grid connections and offtake before construction capital is committed. The read-through is that specialist greenfield platforms remain fundable at scale while generalist energy transition funds struggle.

**Why you care.** Fundraising datapoint for your 'why this fund' prep: it shows where LP capital is still flowing in renewables and how a specialist mandate reads against a generalist one.
