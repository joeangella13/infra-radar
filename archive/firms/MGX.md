### Macquarie completes $40bn exit of Aligned Data Centers to AIP, MGX and GIP consortium after eight-year hold

**Deal / players.** Macquarie Asset Management completed the sale of Aligned Data Centers to a consortium comprising the AI Infrastructure Partnership, MGX and BlackRock's Global Infrastructure Partners. The transaction was announced in October 2025 at a US$40bn enterprise value. Under Macquarie's eight-year ownership from 2018, Aligned grew from two facilities with 85MW to 51 campuses and more than 6.4GW of operational and planned capacity across the Americas; returns to MAM were not disclosed.

### Abu Dhabi's MGX closes near-$50bn raise for AI infrastructure, one of the largest pools ever assembled

`2026-06-23` · Sponsor moves · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/23/mgx-50-billion-ai-tech-abu-dhabi), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/abu-dhabi-s-mgx-raises-about-50-billion-to-accelerate-ai-deals)

**Deal / players.** MGX, the Abu Dhabi AI investment vehicle founded in 2024, raised close to $50bn from sovereign wealth funds and other investors. Existing MGX positions include OpenAI, xAI, Anthropic and ByteDance, alongside its role in the AI Infrastructure Partnership that acquired Aligned Data Centers. Bloomberg characterised it as the first time Abu Dhabi has raised third-party money at this scale rather than deploying its own balance sheet.

**Why it matters.** MGX raising close to $50bn from sovereigns and others turns Abu Dhabi from a captive balance sheet into a fund manager, putting price-insensitive capital up against GIP, Blackstone and Brookfield for the same power and compute assets. Expect entry-multiple pressure on hyperscale platforms and contracted generation. But MGX has tended to write alongside GIP and BlackRock, so the effect may be bigger consortia rather than more bidders.

**Why you care.** Close to $50bn of price-insensitive capital entering the same power and compute assets, so expect entry-multiple pressure, or simply bigger consortia rather than more bidders.
