### Blackstone, Apollo and KKR to invest $5.34bn for 49% of Williams' behind-the-meter Power Innovation JV

`2026-07-13` · Sponsor moves · [Blackstone](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/williams-announces-5-34-billion-investment-in-power-innovation-joint-venture-from-blackstone/)

**Deal / players.** Williams agreed a $5.34bn investment from Blackstone and partners Apollo and KKR for a 49% non-controlling equity interest in its Power Innovation joint venture, with Williams retaining 51% and operational control. The commitment comprises $4.4bn representing 49% of growth capex plus $0.9bn of additional consideration. The JV holds five behind-the-meter projects, Socrates, Apollo, Aquila, Socrates the Younger and Neo, part of Williams' 6GW-plus backlog; Williams holds buyout rights in years seven to fourteen at Blackstone's outstanding investment balance.

**Why it matters.** Structured equity dressed as a JV: Williams keeps 51% and control, and its buyout right in years seven to fourteen at Blackstone's outstanding investment balance caps sponsor upside, which is why three big managers could share the $5.34bn without competing on multiple. Behind-the-meter generation contracted to data centre load is the strongest cash-flow profile in US power now: long tenor, investment-grade counterparties, no merchant risk.

**Why you care.** The structure to know cold, minority structured equity into midstream-sponsored power. Exactly the kind of deal you would be asked to size up at MSIP or Stonepeak.
