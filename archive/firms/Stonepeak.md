### **[watchlist]** Stonepeak exits KAPS, selling remaining 50% NGL pipeline interest to Keyera for C$1.215bn

`2026-06-17` · Sponsor moves · [Stonepeak](https://stonepeak.com/news/keyera-announces-acquisition-of-remaining-50-interest-in-kaps)

**Deal / players.** Keyera Corp. acquired Stonepeak's remaining 50% non-operating interest in the KAPS NGL pipeline system for $1.215bn, taking Keyera to 100% ownership. The transaction closed on announcement. KAPS moves NGL volumes from Montney and Duvernay production to downstream markets, with Zone 4 currently under construction. Keyera guided the deal as accretive to distributable cash flow per share and raised its fee-based adjusted EBITDA per share CAGR outlook for 2025-2027 from 15%-17% to 16%-18%.

**Why it matters.** A clean full exit from a greenfield midstream build, and it closed on announcement, so no financing or regulatory risk hangs over it. Selling to the operating partner is the highest-certainty route for a non-operating stake and usually clears above what a financial sponsor would pay for a passive interest. C$1.215bn for a minority NGL interest is a useful Western Canadian mark.

**Why you care.** Competitor exit worth marking: Stonepeak clearing a non-operating NGL stake to its operating partner at C$1.215bn, closed on announcement, the highest-certainty route out of a minority midstream position.

### **[watchlist]** Stonepeak-led consortium to acquire Estia Health, Australia's second-largest aged care operator, from Bain Capital

`2026-05-27` · Sponsor moves · [Stonepeak](https://stonepeak.com/news/stonepeak-led-consortium-to-acquire-estia-health)

**Deal / players.** A Stonepeak-led consortium agreed to acquire Estia Health from Bain Capital, with Stonepeak taking the majority stake through its Volarae Living platform and Axight Capital Limited taking a minority. Terms undisclosed. Estia operates 90+ residential aged care homes across NSW, Queensland, South Australia and Victoria serving approximately 9,000 residents. Close expected late 2026 subject to regulatory approvals. This is Volarae Living's second ANZ social infrastructure investment after acquiring Aura Holdings in February 2026.

**Why it matters.** Australian aged care earns government per-resident subsidy plus refundable accommodation deposits, so cash flow blends regulated income with a big working-capital float that behaves like cheap financing. Post-Royal Commission reform lifted per-bed subsidies and made the sector investable again; Bain agreeing a sponsor sale rather than a listing says the recovery is priced but not fully harvested. Two ANZ assets in four months means Stonepeak is building a platform.

**Why you care.** A direct competitor building an ANZ social infrastructure platform, two assets in four months, underwriting government subsidy income plus a refundable-deposit float. Terms undisclosed.

### **[watchlist]** Stonepeak to acquire BMO Transportation and Vendor Finance; BMO retains 19.9% minority

`2026-05-11` · Sponsor moves · [Stonepeak](https://stonepeak.com/news/stonepeak-to-acquire-bmo-transportation-and-vendor-finance)

**Deal / players.** Stonepeak agreed to acquire BMO Transportation and Vendor Finance from BMO Financial Group. Terms undisclosed. The Irving, Texas-based platform is one of North America's largest commercial truck and trailer lenders, financing transportation assets and agriculture/construction equipment, with 700+ employees across US and Canadian operations. BMO retains a 19.9% minority interest and the existing leadership team continues. Close expected Q4 2026 subject to regulatory approvals. Stonepeak manages approximately $88bn AUM.

**Why it matters.** Asset-backed leasing rather than hard infrastructure, but the cash flows are contracted lease and loan income against remarketable collateral, a profile Stonepeak has underwritten nine times before across roughly $28bn of EV including Textainer, ATSG and TRAC Intermodal. The lever is funding cost: off a bank balance sheet the platform can use securitisation and private credit, while BMO's retained 19.9% preserves origination flow. Close expected Q4 2026.

**Why you care.** Stonepeak running its asset-backed leasing playbook a ninth time, across roughly $28bn of EV; a direct competitor widening what counts as transport infrastructure. Close expected Q4 2026.
