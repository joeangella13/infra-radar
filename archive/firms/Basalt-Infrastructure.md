### Basalt Infrastructure completes sale of Manx Telecom to CVC DIF and Jersey Telecom consortium

`2026-05-19` · Sponsor moves · [Basalt Infrastructure Partners](https://www.basaltinfra.com/news/basalt-infrastructure-partners-completes-sale-of-manx-telecom/)

**Deal / players.** Basalt Infrastructure Partners LLP completed the sale of Manx Telecom to a consortium of CVC DIF and Jersey Telecom. Terms undisclosed. Manx Telecom is the incumbent telecommunications provider on the Isle of Man, offering fixed line, mobile, broadband and data storage services plus IoT solutions through its OV subsidiary. Basalt acquired the business in 2019. Managing Partner John Hanna said it had been a great experience supporting the company's growth since acquisition.

**Why it matters.** An incumbent island telco is about as defensive as digital infrastructure gets: captive market, no realistic overbuilder, utility-like cash flows. The buyer mix is the tell, with Jersey Telecom bringing an operating playbook from an analogous Crown Dependency and CVC DIF bringing the capital, so the consortium can underwrite synergies a pure financial buyer could not. Seven-year hold, terms undisclosed.

**Why you care.** Outside your sectors, but a good template for exit routing: pairing a strategic with a fund is how you maximise value on a single-market asset.

### Basalt Infrastructure agrees to acquire R.E.L.A.M., North American lessor of rail maintenance-of-way equipment

`2026-05-11` · Sponsor moves · [Basalt Infrastructure Partners](https://www.basaltinfra.com/news/basalt-infrastructure-partners-agrees-to-acquire-r-e-l-a-m-a-leading-north-american-lessor-of-rail-infrastructure-equipment/), [Railway Gazette](https://www.railwaygazette.com/business/2026/05/12/basalt-to-acquire-track-maintenance-plant-leasing-company-relam/)

**Deal / players.** Basalt Infrastructure Partners LLP agreed to acquire R.E.L.A.M., a leading North American lessor of maintenance-of-way and hi-rail equipment, with a fleet of approximately 1,500 specialised rail maintenance assets across the US and Canada. Terms undisclosed and the seller was not named. Close was expected by end of May 2026 subject to customary conditions. William Blair advised on the sale, Holland & Knight acted as legal adviser, and Wells Fargo led debt syndication. Partner Wil Jones cited resilient demand characteristics and strong growth potential.

**Why it matters.** Maintenance-of-way leasing rides non-discretionary track renewal spend by Class I railroads, short lines and contractors, so utilisation holds through freight cycles in a way wagon leasing does not. The plan is fleet growth on cheap infrastructure-style leverage, hence the Wells Fargo debt syndication, plus rolling up a fragmented lessor base. A William Blair auction means entry pricing is probably full.

**Why you care.** Same shape as Jennmar/Weber Mining, an industrial services business sold into a capex cycle, and a clean mid-market rail comp.
