### Argo Infrastructure acquires eight-site C&I solar portfolio from NuGen, taking platform to 270MW

`2026-07-09` · Sponsor moves · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/argo-infrastructure-partners-acquires-solar-portfolio-from-and-establishes-new-partnership-with-owner-developer-nugen-302822094.html)

**Deal / players.** Argo Infrastructure Partners LP acquired an eight-site solar portfolio from NuGen Capital Management, LLC, comprising six sites in Massachusetts and two in New Jersey. Terms undisclosed. The acquisition expands Argo's commercial and industrial solar platform to 270MW across 196 sites. NuGen continues as a partner supporting asset performance post-close. Argo described the deal as its 21st investment since 2013.

**Why it matters.** Argo Infrastructure bought an eight-site C&I solar portfolio from NuGen Capital Management, six sites in Massachusetts and two in New Jersey, terms undisclosed, taking its platform to 270MW across 196 sites and marking its 21st investment since 2013. NuGen stays on as operating partner post-close. Massachusetts SMART and New Jersey SREC-II supply 10-20 year contracted revenue largely insulated from merchant power prices.

**Why you care.** Competitive-set read: a financial owner aggregating C&I solar to 270MW and keeping NuGen on as operator, buying SMART and SREC-II contracted revenue rather than building an asset management team.
