### **[watchlist]** EQT's private equity fund agrees €10.8bn Qiagen take-private with ADIA and CPP Investments co-investing

`2026-10-21` · Sponsor moves · [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/eqt-to-acquire-qiagen-in-e10-8-billion-take-private-with-adia-and-cpp-investments/), [The Enterprise (UAE)](https://enterpriseam.com/uae/2026/10/23/abu-dhabis-sovereign-fund-is-helping-take-another-listed-company-private-alongside-eqt/)

**Deal / players.** The EQT XI fund, through Crown NL Bidco, will offer €46.50 per Qiagen share in cash, a 40.3% premium to the July 8 undisturbed price. Enterprise value is about €10.8bn ($12.1bn), including about $1.1bn of net debt. ADIA (via Luxinva) and CPP Investments co-invest; stake sizes were not disclosed. Qiagen's boards back the offer, and Bloomberg reports KKR also bid.

**Why it matters.** This is a healthcare deal from EQT's private equity fund, not an infrastructure deal, so it says little about infrastructure pricing. It does show EQT pairing with ADIA again after Intertek, and KKR losing out on price. Watch whether the same sovereign co-investors appear on EQT's infrastructure take-privates.

**Why you care.** Background only: this is EQT Private Equity, not EQT Infrastructure, but ADIA and CPP co-invest is the same capital MSIP competes with on large deals.

### **[watchlist]** Stonepeak completes purchase of Chicago Parking Meters from MSIP, Allianz and an ADIA subsidiary

`2026-10-21` · Sponsor moves · [Stonepeak release via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/stonepeak-completes-acquisition-chicago-parking-214500100.html), [MSIP release via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/morgan-stanley-infrastructure-partners-completes-215000261.html), [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/stonepeak-closes-2-53b-deal-for-chicago-s-parking-meters)

**Deal / players.** Stonepeak said on Oct 21 it has completed its acquisition of Chicago Parking Meters, which runs the city's on-street parking system (38,000+ metered spaces, nearly 10,000 downtown). The sellers were a consortium of Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners, Allianz and a wholly owned ADIA subsidiary. The two releases I read give no price; Dealroom reports $2.53bn, 57 years left on the lease, and a Chicago City Council approval of 46-3 on Sept 29.

**Why it matters.** A 57-year city lease paid from metered-parking fees is a core-style concession. MSIP says it grew the business through new pay stations, a 24-hour customer centre and the ParkChicago app. Stonepeak's core team is buying a modernised, mature asset, so the price (if confirmed) is a comp for city-lease deals. Watch for confirmed terms and the changes the council negotiated.

**Why you care.** An MSIP exit to a direct rival, closed before you start: a finished example of the firm's modernise-then-sell playbook, and a possible price marker for any concession MSIP looks at next.
