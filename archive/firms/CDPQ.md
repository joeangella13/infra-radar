### La Caisse agrees to buy 49.9% of Bell's new TowerCo for C$2.45bn, valuing it at about C$4.91bn

`2026-10-27` · Sponsor moves · [BCE press release (Newswire.ca)](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/bce-announces-partnership-with-la-caisse-which-will-acquire-49-9-stake-in-bell-wireless-tower-infrastructure-company-822276026.html), [Investing.com (Reuters)](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/bce-sells-499-stake-in-tower-unit-to-la-caisse-for-c245b-93CH-4974849), [Investment Executive](https://www.investmentexecutive.com/news/industry/bce-sells-49-9-stake-in-wireless-tower-company-to-la-caisse-for-2-45b/)

**Deal / players.** BCE's Bell Canada signed a definitive agreement to sell 49.9% of a newly formed tower company to La Caisse for C$2.45bn, subject to adjustments. TowerCo will hold more than 5,000 Canadian sites, and Bell keeps 50.1% control. TowerCo is expected to have no financial debt at closing; closing is targeted for Q1 2027 pending regulatory approvals.

**Why it matters.** Bell leases the towers back under long-term agreements, so day-one cash flow is anchor-tenant rent, and the upside is new builds, densification and third-party colocation. Bell uses the cash to cut debt toward 3.5x leverage by end-2027. It follows Telus's C$1.26bn tower sale to the same buyer last year. Multiples were not disclosed; watch for them at closing.

**Why you care.** A fresh, large minority-tower comp with an anchor lease-back and zero debt at close, useful when marking digital infrastructure carve-outs against the telco deleveraging pipeline.
