### **[watchlist]** I Squared Capital marks completed public listing of its Cube Highways Trust InvIT in India

`2026-08-12` · Sponsor moves · [I Squared Capital](https://isquaredcapital.com/), [IndMoney](https://www.indmoney.com/ipo/cube-highways-trust-ipo), [Zerodha](https://zerodha.com/ipo/453059/cube-highways-trust/)

**Deal / players.** I Squared Capital announced completion of the public listing of Cube Highways Trust, an Indian road InvIT it co-sponsors alongside BCI and Mubadala (via Seventy Second). The trust holds 27 operational highway assets covering 8,754 lane kilometres across 12 states, run under toll, annuity and hybrid-annuity concessions.

**Why it matters.** Taking a private toll-road platform public via India's InvIT structure is a proven exit route for mid-market infrastructure sponsors to recycle capital while keeping a stake, and it gives I Squared a fresh public comp for its own road book. It also confirms Indian InvIT demand is intact even after a lukewarm early subscription.

**Why you care.** Shows how a direct competitor monetizes mature transport assets through public listing rather than a trade sale, a route MSIP could weigh for its own infrastructure exits.
