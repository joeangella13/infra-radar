### Hydrostor says Willow Rock is nearing groundbreaking later this year with a signed project labor agreement and Hatch as EPC-M

`2026-07-09` · Tracked situations · [Hydrostor](https://hydrostor.ca/open-houses-for-local-contractors-and-suppliers-show-growing-momentum-for-willow-rock-energy-storage-center/)

**Deal / players.** Hydrostor said the 500 MW, eight-hour Willow Rock Energy Storage Center in Kern County, California is nearing groundbreaking and construction later this year, with a project labor agreement already signed and Hatch acting as EPC-M partner while Hydrostor contracts directly with vendors and suppliers. The company hosted contractor and supplier open houses in Rosamond and Bakersfield, reporting interest from more than 40 local vendors. It cited over $500m of direct and indirect benefits to Kern County and approximately $18m in annual property taxes, with around 40 permanent operating positions.

**Why it matters.** A signed project labor agreement and supplier outreach drawing more than 40 local vendors are pre-mobilisation activities, so this is a real construction-start signal. Under the EPC-M structure Hatch manages but Hydrostor contracts vendors directly, leaving procurement risk with the sponsor and making the financing package the gating item. Willow Rock anchors the DOE conditional commitment of up to $1.76bn and the 50 MW California Community Power offtake, so a slip reprices the platform.

**Why you care.** Hydrostor at its sharpest point: whether groundbreaking actually happens in 2026, and whether financial close lands with it, is what marks the platform.

### Hydrostor signs 50 MW Willow Rock offtake with California Community Power on behalf of six CCAs

`2026-02-12` · Tracked situations · [Hydrostor](https://hydrostor.ca/hydrostor-signs-offtake-agreement-with-california-community-power/)

**Deal / players.** Hydrostor signed a 50 MW offtake agreement for the Willow Rock Energy Storage Center with California Community Power acting for six member community choice aggregators: CleanPowerSF, Peninsula Clean Energy, Redwood Coast Energy Authority, San Jose Clean Energy, Silicon Valley Clean Energy Authority and Valley Clean Energy Authority. Term length was not disclosed. The project received its California Energy Commission permit in December 2025 and a franchise agreement from the Kern County Board of Supervisors, and is expected online in 2030.

**Why it matters.** Contracted capacity is what lets lenders size debt, so this is the milestone that makes the DOE conditional commitment financeable. But 50 MW against 500 MW of nameplate means only a tenth of the project is sold; the rest needs more offtake or merchant CAISO exposure. The six-CCA counterparty group is fragmented, which lenders will scrutinise.

**Why you care.** You worked Hydrostor, so this is the number that anchors Willow Rock's contracted share, and the gap you would have to defend in any valuation.
