**Deal / players.** McDermott priced $550m of senior bonds at a 10.5% coupon maturing July 2031, with application to be made for listing on Euronext Nordic ABM. Net proceeds will be applied toward cash collateralising letter of credit facilities, refinancing existing debt and general corporate purposes. The bond forms part of a broader refinancing alongside a $500m equity rights offering fully backstopped by major shareholders, with an ex-rights date of July 10, record date and special shareholder meeting on July 13, and a subscription period expiring August 17, 2026 at 4:59 p.m. ET. **Why it matters.** A 10.5% coupon on senior paper, against $489m of trailing EBITDA and a net cash balance sheet, says the market still prices McDermott off its restructuring history. Use of proceeds is the tell: cash collateralising LC facilities is a bonding fix for a contractor that cannot get uncollateralised capacity. Over $1bn raised in total, including $500m of dilutive equity, with subscription expiring August 17 and Q3 closing expected.
