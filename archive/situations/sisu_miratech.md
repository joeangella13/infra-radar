**Deal / players.** MIRATECH completed the acquisition of Advanced Catalyst Systems, a Maryville, Tennessee manufacturer of high-performance catalyst technologies and emission control systems for power generation and industrial sectors, with terms undisclosed. The combination expands catalyst production capacity across power generation, gas compression and industrial applications and integrates operations in the East Tennessee area. CEO David Zenthoefer said the deal allows MIRATECH to meet growing demand for its proprietary solutions. **Why it matters.** Buying a catalyst manufacturer rather than a distributor moves MIRATECH into the highest-margin, most supply-constrained part of emissions aftertreatment. Catalyst lead times have been a bottleneck for distributed generation serving data centres, so owning production is a competitive weapon. With ECI six weeks later, that is a pattern of capability-led buys across geography and the value chain. Terms undisclosed on both, so deployed capital and sponsor stay unconfirmed.
