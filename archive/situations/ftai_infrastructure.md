### FTAI Infrastructure agrees to sell Long Ridge Energy and Power to MARA Holdings for about $1.52bn, targeting Q3 2026 close

`2026-04-30` · Tracked situations · [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/30/3284709/0/en/ftai-infrastructure-inc-announces-agreement-to-sell-long-ridge-energy-and-power-to-mara-holdings-inc.html)

**Deal / players.** FTAI Infrastructure agreed to sell Long Ridge Energy & Power LLC and related assets to a subsidiary of MARA Holdings for approximately $1.52bn before closing adjustments. The assets include a 485 MW combined cycle gas power plant, working interests in natural gas production wells and roughly 1,600 acres along the Ohio River in southeastern Ohio. Closing is expected in the third quarter of 2026 subject to regulatory approvals and customary conditions; FIP said it will eliminate $1.16bn of Long Ridge debt and use net proceeds to repay approximately $300m of corporate debt and reinvest in freight rail and terminals. Jefferies and Lazard advised, with Skadden Arps as legal counsel.

**Why it matters.** FTAI Infrastructure agreed to sell Long Ridge Energy & Power, including a 485 MW combined-cycle gas plant, to a MARA Holdings subsidiary for about $1.52bn, with closing expected in Q3 2026 subject to regulatory approvals. FIP will eliminate $1.16bn of Long Ridge debt, repay roughly $300m of corporate debt and reinvest the rest in freight rail and terminals. The buyer is a bitcoin miner pivoting to AI and data centre power.

**Why you care.** You ran FTAI Infrastructure. Long Ridge agreed at about $1.52bn to a bitcoin miner pivoting to AI power, closing expected Q3 2026, the gas-to-AI thesis clearing at a price.
