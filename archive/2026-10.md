The aftermarket service tail is the recurring piece. Watch whether the developer later brings in an owner or financier for the units.

**Why you care.** A fresh demand signal for off-grid gas power, the same gas-to-AI theme as Sisu/Miratech; equipment lead times are a risk to flag if MSIP underwrites behind-the-meter power.
