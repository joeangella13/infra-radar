### KKR closes largest-ever infrastructure fund at $19.2bn

`2026-08-03` · Market / regulatory · [Investing.com](https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/kkr-closes-192-billion-infrastructure-fund-93CH-4770582)

The seed portfolio — power, fiber and data infrastructure — signals where KKR sees the best risk-adjusted returns right now. Watch how fast the remaining roughly $10bn of dry powder gets deployed into power and digital deals.
